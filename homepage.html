Frequently Asked Questions

How does solar energy work?

Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity. The system design determines how the electricity is used and whether additional equipment is needed.

Can solar panels help lower electricity bills?

They may reduce electricity costs depending on your energy use, system size, sunlight, installation cost, and your electricity provider's rules.

Do solar panels work on cloudy days?

Yes. Solar panels can still generate electricity in cloudy conditions, but usually produce less power than on sunny days.
